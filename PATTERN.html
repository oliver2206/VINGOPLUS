How to Use

  • Purple Boxes:Enter your chosen numbers in the exact positions you want them
  • White Boxes:The system will automatically generate valid numbers for these positions
  • FREE Space:The center cell (N column, 3rd row) is always FREE and cannot be changed
  • B Column:Numbers 1-15 | I Column:Numbers 16-30
  • N Column:Numbers 31-45 | G Column:Numbers 46-60
  • O Column:Numbers 61-75
  • Click "Generate Cards" to create multiple cards with your fixed numbers in the same positions
  • Your numbers (purple) stay the same across all cards, only auto-generated numbers (white) change
  • Click "Reshuffle Cards" to generate new auto-generated numbers while keeping your numbers in place
